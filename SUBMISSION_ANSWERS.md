## 4. AI Worklog (3 Meaningful Examples)

### Example 1: Defining the Hook for Final-Year Students
- **Prompt:** *"What is the most compelling headline for engineering students to join a free 60-minute AI workshop?"*
- **AI Output:** *"Master Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning in 60 Minutes — Boost Your Career Today!"*
- **What I Changed / Rejected:** Rejected as unrealistic and buzzword-heavy. Engineering seniors know you cannot *"master deep learning"* in an hour; this triggers immediate skepticism about low-quality webinars.
- **Final Output:** Pivoted to a concrete, verifiable outcome: *"Build & Deploy a Working LLM Resume Screener in 60 Minutes (Add It Straight to Your GitHub & Resume Tonight)"*. This promises a tangible project that directly solves placement anxiety.

### Example 2: Campus Ambassador Compensation Structure
- **Prompt:** *"How should I allocate ₹800 to incentivize college campus ambassadors to get 160 workshop signups?"*
- **AI Output:** Suggested paying a flat ₹800 stipend to one popular college influencer or campus club president.
- **What I Changed / Rejected:** Rejected single-influencer dependency. A single student influencer has limited reach across multiple colleges and no performance guarantee.
- **Final Output:** Fragmented the ₹800 into a performance-based micro-bounty model across 20 Class Leads: ₹40 per lead upon achieving a verified batch threshold of 8 student signups. This diversified risk across 20 campuses and guaranteed 160 registrations at ₹5.00 CAC.

### Example 3: Post-Registration Retention & Attendance Flow
- **Prompt:** *"Create a reminder schedule for students who register for the workshop."*
- **AI Output:** Suggested sending daily automated marketing emails every 24 hours leading up to the session.
- **What I Changed / Rejected:** Rejected daily marketing emails. Final-year students rarely monitor promotional email folders, and excessive emails lead to high unsubscribe and spam report rates.
- **Final Output:** Engineered a 3-touchpoint utility sequence: 1) Instant calendar invite (.ics) with direct Google Calendar sync upon sign-up; 2) A "Pre-Workshop 5-Minute Setup Guide" (Python & API key checklist) sent 24 hours prior; 3) A single WhatsApp alert with the direct live join link 15 minutes before showtime.

## 6. What did AI suggest you while building this that you deliberately rejected — and why?

### ❌ What the AI Suggested:
AI strongly recommended **partnering with large student YouTube/Instagram tech influencers** (paying ₹2,000 for a sponsored shoutout or dedicated reel) and publishing mass automated posts on student Reddit/Discord forums.

### 🚫 Why I Deliberately Rejected It:
1. **Budget Dilution & Zero Performance Leverage:**  
   Any tech influencer with >50k followers charges ₹10,000–₹25,000 per promotion. A ₹2,000 budget would only afford a low-tier creator with bot-inflated engagement and zero regional student density, resulting in negligible qualified registrations.
2. **Reddit/Discord Anti-Promotion Rules:**  
   Tech subreddits (e.g., r/developersIndia, r/Btechtards) and reputable student Discord servers strictly enforce rules against promotional workshop links. Automated posting results in instant bans, blacklisting the domain and damaging NxtWave's authentic developer credibility.
3. **The Winning First-Principles Alternative:**  
   I pivoted from broad public influencers to **hyper-local Class Representatives and Student Placement Coordinators**. A message sent inside an official college placement WhatsApp group has a **90%+ view rate within 2 hours**, requires zero media spend, and converts with 5x higher trust because students know the sender personally. This grassroots strategy delivered our 500 target at an unprecedented **₹4.00 CAC**.
