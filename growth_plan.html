5. Three Core Growth Hypotheses & Experiments

Test 1 (Placement Coordinator Endorsement vs. Peer Forwarding):
Hypothesis: Forwarding via official Student Placement Cell representatives with a formal endorsement will generate 3x higher registration conversion than peer-to-peer forwarded messages.
Metric: Registration link conversion rate (CVR) and attendance confirmation rate.

Test 2 (Working Code Screen Recording vs. Graphic Flyer):
Hypothesis: A 15-second screen recording showing the finished AI Resume Screener working in real-time will generate 65%+ higher CTR on WhatsApp than a polished graphic banner.
Metric: Click-through rate (CTR) to the registration landing page.

Test 3 (Inter-Branch Rivalry Leaderboard vs. Generic Referrals):
Hypothesis: Introducing an inter-branch rivalry mechanic ("CSE vs. IT vs. ECE: Which department builds more AI projects?") will increase referral frequency by 80% over standard prompts.
Metric: Viral expansion factor (K) and referral velocity per active registrant.

6. Strategic Rejection: Why Paid Influencer Sponsorships Were Discarded

AI Recommendation: Pay ₹2,000 to a mid-tier student YouTuber/Instagram tech influencer for a dedicated promo reel.

First-Principles Rejection: Any credible tech creator charges ₹10,000–₹25,000. A ₹2,000 budget only affords low-tier creators with bot-inflated views and negligible college density. Instead, decentralizing into 20 Class Leads generated 160 verified registrations at ₹5.00 CAC with 10x higher trust.
